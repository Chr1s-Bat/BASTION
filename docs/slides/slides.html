01
Opening

The problem we set out to solve

Today

  • Requests live on paper and spreadsheets
  • Stock counts drift out of date
  • Approvals get lost between offices
  • Nobody can say who holds which equipment

Goals for this system

  • One live record of every item, by depot
  • A clear request → approve → issue → return trail
  • Low-stock warnings before shortages hurt
  • Right people see and do the right things
02
Solution overview

Two roles, one workflow

Supply Officer

  • Registers equipment
  • Files requisitions, edits while pending
  • Tracks their own requests

Base Commander

  • Approves, rejects, issues, closes out
  • Sees every request and the personnel roster
  • Manages equipment status and deletions

A requisition's life

Pending➜ Approved➜ Issued➜ Returned or Rejected

Issuing takes units out of stock; returning puts them back. Everything also works on a phone.

03
Solution overview

How it works, in plain language

  • The screen (React) is what people see and click
  • The rules desk (Laravel API) checks who you are and what you may do
  • The filing cabinet (MySQL) keeps every record

Sign-in gives each person a private token. Every request carries it, so the system always knows who is asking and what they are allowed to do.

5 tables

Users · Categories · Depots · Items · Requisitions. An item belongs to one category and one depot; a requisition links an officer to an item.
